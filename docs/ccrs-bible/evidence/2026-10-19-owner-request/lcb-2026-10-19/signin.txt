User Sign-In Guide
How to access CCRS with the new WA.gov sign-in




CIB154 8/26
Welcome
The Cannabis Central Reporting System (CCRS) is where Washington State cannabis licensees
submit required reporting data. CCRS now uses Washington State's WA.gov sign-in to keep your
account secure.
This short guide explains how to sign in and get set up. It applies to licensees and their employees who
upload files through the CCRS website. Users can follow the same steps to access the CCRS Pre-
Production website.
Note: if you connect to CCRS using the API, this guide is not for you. Please see the separate CCRS
Integrator API Guide.

What’s changing?
Previously you signed in with a CCRS-specific login. Now you'll sign in using a WA.gov account - the
same secure sign-in used across many Washington State services. The first time you sign in, you'll
complete a quick one-time registration so CCRS knows which license(s) your WA.gov account is tied
to.
After the one-time setup, you simply sign in with WA.gov each time. You won't need to register again.

Getting Started
Step 1 - Make sure you have a WA.gov account
You need a WA.gov account to sign in to CCRS. If you already use one for other Washington State
services, you can use it here. You will need separate WA.gov accounts for the Pre-Production
environment and the live environment.
   •   Create a WA.gov account here
   •   Create a Pre-Production WA.gov account here

Step 2 - Sign in to CCRS
 1. Go to the CCRS website.
 2. Choose to sign in. You'll be taken to the WA.gov sign-in page.




 3. Sign in with your WA.gov account.

Step 3 - Complete the one-time registration
The first time you sign in with WA.gov, CCRS needs to link your WA.gov account to your CCRS
account. You'll be taken to a short registration screen. This is a one-time step, and future visits will only
require your WA.gov login information.




  1. Enter the email address you use for CCRS reporting. This links your WA.gov sign-in to your CCRS
     account. This email address must already have been set up with LCB Licensing.


  2. Click “Register.” You're now set up, can access CCRS, and submit files as usual.


Need Help?
