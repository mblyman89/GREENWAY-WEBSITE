**Subject:** Question: automating our own CCRS file uploads for license 413541 (Greenway Marijuana)

Hello Brian,

Thank you again for all your help while we moved off Cultivera and became our own uploader. We now upload our weekly files ourselves through the CCRS upload page, and they are being accepted.

We have one question before we change how we do it.

Production emails its answer a few hours after each upload. To keep our records in the right order, we upload in three groups and wait for every email before starting the next group:

1. Strain, Area and Product.
2. Inventory.
3. Adjustments, Transfers and Sales.

Done by hand, that means a person has to come back to the computer several times a day.

We would like to automate those clicks. This is how it would work:

- I log in to CCRS myself, with my own WA.gov account, in my own browser. Our system never sees or stores my password.
- A small browser add-on we built uploads our own CSV files through the same CCRS upload page, one file at a time. These are the same files, in the format the CCRS specification describes, that we upload by hand today.
- It sends the next group only after CCRS's email for the earlier group comes back with success.
- If CCRS refuses anything, or shows a page our system does not recognize, it stops and alerts me. It never re-sends a file on its own.
- I start each run myself, and I can stop it at any time.

We are not asking for integrator or API access with this. It is the same login and the same upload page, without someone having to wait at the keyboard. If you would rather we keep uploading by hand until we are approved as an integrator, we will do that.

**Our questions**

1. May a licensee upload its own CSV files this way, through the CCRS web upload page and under its own WA.gov login, with a browser add-on doing the clicking?
2. Is there a limit on how many files we should upload in a day, or a minimum time between uploads, that you would like us to keep to?
3. If the answer is no, is there another way you would suggest for us, or is the integrator application the right path?

Thank you for your time. We want to be sure we do this the way the LCB expects.
