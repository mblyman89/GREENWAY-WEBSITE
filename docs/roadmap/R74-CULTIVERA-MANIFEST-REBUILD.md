## S19 behavior (read faster per press, owner round 10)

- **Why it was slow.** A press read at most 12 documents, strictly one after another; each PDF is one LlamaParse job (upload, then a status poll every 2 s until done, often 5 to 40 s). Twelve in a row took about a minute.
- **What a press does now.** Five workers share one list of up to 100 stored documents (newest email first). - **Order.** Newest email first (`email_at desc`, then id). The owner saw "starts at the very last one"; the current shelf came on the latest manifests, so a part-read mailbox already places today's lots.
- **Fail loud.** A storage outage stops every worker from starting another document; the ones in flight are written; the outage is named in the result; the rest stay `stored`. A document another press already wrote is not counted (the guarded update is still the claim).
