SOURCE ARTIFACTS - IRS BLANK FORMS 941 AND W-2, AS PUBLISHED
Used as the ARTWORK and the FIELD GEOMETRY for the on-screen facsimiles

Mirrored in books-61 because the product now renders the agency's own page and
positions every value using the agency's own rectangles. Standing rule 115: a
claim about where a box sits on a tax form must rest on a document in the
repository, not on a measurement quoted in a conversation.

The sha256 of each is recorded so that a future regeneration can prove it used
the SAME artwork. If the IRS reissues either form the hash changes, the derived
geometry changes with it, and the change is then a deliberate act in a commit
that says so - rather than a silent shift in where figures print on a document
that gets filed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT WAS DERIVED FROM THEM, AND HOW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Nothing about the layout is typed by hand. Two scripts read the PDFs:

  scripts/derive-form-geometry.py
    Reads the AcroForm dictionary with pypdf and records, for every /Widget
    annotation on the pages we render: its /Rect, its field /FT, its /Q
    alignment (0 left, 1 centre, 2 right), its /MaxLen and its /Ff flags.
    Emits src/lib/payroll/form-geometry.generated.json.

    Two conversions are performed and are worth stating because both are easy
    to get silently wrong:

      - PDF's origin is the BOTTOM-left of the page; CSS's is the TOP-left.
        So y_css = pageHeight - y1.

      - /Ff bit 25 (1 << 24) is the COMB flag. A comb field is divided into
        /MaxLen equal cells, one character per cell. It is why Michael's own
        filed 941 prints his EIN as "4 6 - 4 2 1 7 0 1 6". A field flagged comb
        with NO /MaxLen has no cell count, and the script refuses it rather
        than choosing one.

    The page artwork itself is converted with `pdftocairo -svg` into
    public/forms/art/, so the lines, captions and shading a reader sees are the
    agency's own vector drawing rather than a redrawing of it.

  scripts/derive-form-box-map.py
    Binds each rectangle to the box our engines model, by reading the PRINTED
    text near it with `pdftotext -bbox-layout` - the line numbers in the left
    margin, and, in the entity area, the captions themselves. Emits
    src/lib/payroll/form-box-map.generated.json.

    Every rectangle must end up either BOUND to a box or listed as
    deliberately blank WITH A WRITTEN REASON. There is no third state. A
    rectangle that is merely absent from both lists is what an unfilled EIN box
    looked like, and the script exits rather than emitting one.

CORROBORATED AGAINST A RETURN THE IRS ACCEPTED
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Standing rule 115: a filed return outranks my reasoning. Greenway's own
2ND_QTR_FORM_941.pdf was read with `pdftotext -layout` and shows:

  EIN         4 6 - 4 2 1 7 0 1 6     (one digit per comb cell)
  Name        LYMAN'S MARIJUANA        (the name the EIN was issued to)
  Trade name  GREENWAY MARIJUANA       (a SEPARATE box - see below)
  Address     4851 GEIGER RD SE
              PORT ORCHARD WA 98366
  Signature   MICHAEL LYMAN / OWNER

  Line 16's monthly grid: BLANK on the filed return.
  The entire paid-preparer block: BLANK on the filed return.

The legal-name / trade-name distinction is load-bearing rather than cosmetic.
The IRS matches a return to an account on the EIN plus the NAME CONTROL - the
first four characters of the name the EIN was issued to. Greenway trades as
GREENWAY MARIJUANA but the EIN belongs to LYMAN'S MARIJUANA, so printing the
trading name in the name box is a mismatch, and a mismatched return does not
post to the right account.
