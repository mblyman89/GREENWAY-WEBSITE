15 U.S. Code SS 1674 - Restriction on discharge from employment by reason of
garnishment

(a) Termination of employment

No employer may discharge any employee by reason of the fact that his earnings
have been subjected to garnishment for any one indebtedness.

(b) Penalties

Whoever willfully violates subsection (a) of this section shall be fined not
more than $1,000, or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

(Pub. L. 90-321, title III, SS 304, May 29, 1968, 82 Stat. 163.)
