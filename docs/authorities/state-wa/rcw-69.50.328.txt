RCW 69.50.328 

Cannabis producers, processors — No direct or indirect financial interest in licensed cannabis retailers. 

Neither a licensed cannabis producer nor a licensed cannabis processor shall have a direct or indirect financial interest in a licensed cannabis retailer.
