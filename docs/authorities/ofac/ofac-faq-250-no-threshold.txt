250. Does OFAC recommend a specific match threshold score?
Answer
OFAC cannot make such a recommendation because each search has its own unique set of facts surrounding it. Users of
Sanctions List Search
must make their own match threshold determinations based upon their own internal risk assessments and established compliance practices.
Date Released
June 9, 2014
