   850-10-05-1            The Related Party Disclosures Topic provides disclosure requirements for related party transactions
                          and certain common control relationships. Accounting and reporting issues concerning certain related
                          party transactions and relationships are addressed in other Topics.

   850-10-05-2            [ Information about transactions with related parties is useful in comparing an entity's results of
                          operations and financial position with those of prior periods and with those of other entities. It helps
                          users of financial statements to detect and explain possible differences. [ FAS 057 18 47.1 ] ]

   850-10-05-3            [ Examples of related party transactions include those between: [ FAS 057 1 9.2.1 ] ]
                              a. [ A parent entity and its subsidiaries [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.1 ] ]
                              b. [ Subsidiaries of a common parent [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                              c. [ An entity and trusts for the benefit of employees, such as pension and profit-sharing trusts
                               that are managed by or under the trusteeship of the entity's management [
                               FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               d. [ An entity and its principal owners, management, or members of their immediate families [
                               FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               e. [ Affiliates. [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]


   850-10-05-4            [ Transactions between related parties commonly occur in the normal course of business. Examples
                          of common transactions with related parties are: [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               a. [ Sales, purchases, and transfers of real and personal property [
                               FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               b. [ Services received or furnished, such as accounting, management, engineering, and legal
                               services [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               c. [ Use of property and equipment by lease or otherwise [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               d. [ Borrowings, lendings, and guarantees [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               e. [ Maintenance of compensating bank balances for the benefit of a related party [
                               FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               f. [ Intra-entity billings based on allocations of common costs [
                               FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]
                               g. [ Filings of consolidated tax returns. [ FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.1 ] ]


   850-10-05-5            [ Transactions between related parties are considered to be related party transactions even though
                          they may not be given accounting recognition. For example, an entity may receive services from a
                          related party without charge and not record receipt of the services. While not providing accounting or
                          measurement guidance for such transactions, this Topic requires their disclosure nonetheless. [
                          FAS 057 1 9.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2.2 ] ]

   850-10-50-1            [ Financial statements shall include disclosures of material related party transactions, other than
                          compensation arrangements, expense allowances, and other similar items in the ordinary course of
                          business. [ FAS 057 2 11.1 ] ] [ However, disclosure of transactions that are eliminated in the
                          preparation of consolidated or combined financial statements is not required in those statements.
                          The disclosures shall include: [ FAS 057 2 11.2 ] ]
                               a. [ The nature of the relationship(s) involved [ FAS 057 2 14 ] ]
                               b. [ A description of the transactions, including transactions to which no amounts or nominal

                               such other information deemed necessary to an understanding of the effects of the transactions
                               on the financial statements [ FAS 057 2 15 ] ]
                               c. [ The dollar amounts of transactions for each of the periods for which income statements are
                               presented and the effects of any change in the method of establishing the terms from that used
                               in the preceding period [ FAS 057 2 16 ] ]
                               d. [ Amounts due from or to related parties as of the date of each balance sheet presented and,
                               if not otherwise apparent, the terms and manner of settlement [ FAS 057 2 17 ] ]
                               e. [ The information required by paragraph 740-10-50-17. [ FAS 057 2 18 ] ]
